華語 words' 四川話 readings (FangfangCards/zh_sc.json)

Built on your own computer by build-data.sh (tools/build_sichuan.py); not included in this repository.
- Wiktionary (via kaikki.org wiktextract), CC BY-SA 4.0: the 四川話 (成都) readings in 四川話拼音 of pages with the
  same characters and the same 普通話 reading as a word of the 華語 dictionary.
- The other words' readings are put together from how each character is read in those words (marked as guessed
  in the app), or predicted from the regular sound changes from 普通話 learned from those characters, with the old
  入聲 characters spotted from the 粵語 data (marked as predicted). Kept in a file of its own; the dictionary's data
  isn't changed.
- 四川話's own words (not in the 華語 dictionary) and the 華語 words they stand for: the 成都 rows of Wiktionary's
  dialect tables, and pages with meanings marked Sichuanese (their English meanings).
