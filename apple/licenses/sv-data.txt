瑞典語 (Swedish) data used by Fangfang Cards
=============================================

None of this data is in the repository. The app downloads the original files and builds sv.json on the
device (FangfangCards/Install/swedish.js); install.sh builds the same file on the Mac for the Safari
extension (tools/build_swedish.py).

1. Folkets lexikon (Swedish-English), KTH Royal Institute of Technology (Viggo Kann, Joachim Hollman)
   http://folkets-lexikon.csc.kth.se/folkets/
   License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.5 (CC BY-SA 2.5)
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.5/
   Downloaded from a copy on GitHub (antonlisq/convert-dict), or from KTH.
   Used: the words, word classes, English translations, definitions, examples, idioms, inflected forms,
   synonyms and the compound boundaries ("|").

2. Wiktionary (Chinese edition), Swedish entries, extracted by kaikki.org (Tatu Ylonen, Wiktextract)
   https://kaikki.org/zhwiktionary/
   License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0) and GFDL
   Used: the Chinese meanings of Swedish words.

3. FrequencyWords (Hermit Dave), from OpenSubtitles 2018
   https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
   License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0)
   Used: how common each word is (the order and groups of the word list).

4. CC-CEDICT (MDBG), https://cc-cedict.org/
   License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0)
   Used: for words without Chinese meanings, Mandarin words whose English meaning is the word's English one
   (shown marked as guessed, and only when the setting is on), read the other way round.

5. rime-essay (Rime), https://github.com/rime/rime-essay
   License: LGPL-3.0
   Used: how common each Mandarin word is, to pick the guessed ones.

The morpheme splits (root, prefix, suffix, linking letter, ending) are made by this project's rules and the
explanations of prefixes and suffixes are this project's own.
